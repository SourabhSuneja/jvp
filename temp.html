Final Skill Task

ON AIR
Record a 30-Second Podcast

Every student plans, records and edits one short audio clip in TwistedWave, then exports it as their finished piece — a true skill-based showcase of everything learned.

Skill Checklist

Explains what audio & sound waves are

Trims and fades a clip in TwistedWave
Speaks clearly and confidently on mic

Equipment Needed

USB Microphone
  • USB plug-and-play — no extra interface needed
  • Cardioid condenser mic for clear, focused voice pickup
  • Desktop stand + foam windscreen included
  • Works with Windows, macOS & Chromebook
  • One unit per recording station (shared in small groups)
Medium budget unit required
